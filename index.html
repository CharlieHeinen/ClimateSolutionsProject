Data Center Simulation

You've just been appointed as the CEO of a new AI company! To support the growing needs of your customers, you've decided to build a new data center. But since you're a good person who cares about the environment more than profits, you want to do what you can to limit emissions. Adjust the settings to represent a typical AI data center, and then try to get your total CO2 emissions as low as possible. If you have questions, try asking the "AI" model below!

Hint: most data centers get over 55% of their energy from fossil fuels. Try to find a way to get power from clean sources instead!

Summary:

AI is here to stay. As models improve and needs for data grow, more data centers are going to be built, and it is important to limit emissions from them as much as possible. One of the biggest sources of emissions from data centers is from the fossil fuels used to power them. Requiring data centers to get most of their energy from clean sources will significantly reduce their CO2 emissions and impact on climate change. In addition, having IDAs provide financial incentives like tax cuts will encourage data centers to follow these regulations instead of moving their data centers to other locations. Creating these regulations will require support from the public to get lawmakers to support them. You can help by going to your local government and pushing for more data center regulations in your area, or contacting state lawmakers to get their support bills for to increase regulations.
